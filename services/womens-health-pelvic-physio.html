Understanding Pelvic Floor Health

The pelvic floor is a vital hammock of muscles supporting your bladder, uterus, and bowel. Pregnancy, childbirth, hormonal shifts, or high-impact stress can cause muscle weakness or hypertonicity (over-tightness).

Our university-trained female physiotherapists perform discrete assessments in soundproof private rooms. Using Real-Time Ultrasound, we visualize your pelvic floor activation live on screen to build a personalized strength & relaxation program.
