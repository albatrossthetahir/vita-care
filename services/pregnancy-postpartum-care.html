Pregnancy & Postpartum Rehabilitation

Supporting your body through pregnancy pain, birth preparation, abdominal separation (DRAM) checks, and safe postnatal return-to-fitness.

Comprehensive Maternal Care

During pregnancy, the hormone relaxin softens ligaments, which can cause severe pelvic girdle pain (PGP), pubic symphysis dysfunction, or sciatica. After birth, checking for abdominal separation (DRAM) and restoring core strength is essential before resuming heavy exercise.
