Musculoskeletal & Spine Physiotherapy

Relieve chronic lower back pain, neck tension, sciatica, and joint stiffness through hands-on manual mobilization and biomechanical realignment.

Restoring Spinal Alignment & Mobility

Musculoskeletal physiotherapy targets the complex interactions between bones, muscles, ligaments, and nerves. Poor desk posture, heavy lifting, or pelvic misalignment can trigger severe nerve compression and chronic pain.

We use a combination of spinal mobilization, dry needling, myofascial trigger point release, and targeted postural exercise to treat the root cause rather than masking symptoms.
